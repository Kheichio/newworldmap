How to play

  • Actions. You get 1 action per turn, plus 1 for every two cities (up to 3). Use the quick actions (they highlight every valid tile) or click any tile and choose from its panel. Press Space to end your turn early. When your actions are spent, every rival moves.
  • Land only produces when worked — within 2 tiles of one of your settlements. Hatched tiles are yours but idle: found a village nearby to put them to work.
  • Food feeds your people; the surplus fills the growth bar and adds citizens. Villages become towns (4 people) and cities (9). A shortfall causes famine.
  • Materials build improvements, roads, settlements and castles. Gold pays for expansion, trade, war, edicts and upkeep. Costs rise as your nation grows.
  • Resources give a taste of their yield; the matching improvement (pasture, mine, lumber camp, farm, fishery) unlocks the full yield. Ruins 🏺 reward whoever claims them.
  • Borders grow by themselves around settlements every turn. Expand grabs a specific tile now. Villages can be founded on free land touching your border.
  • Settlement limit. Your capital supports 3 settlements; each town adds 1 and each city adds 2. Grow and upgrade before you spread.
  • Fortune cards. Every turn you draw 3 cards and may play one for free. Some pay out at once (gold, materials, growth); others discount a kind of action for the rest of the turn. Your nation's colour and your leader's trait make related cards more likely.
  • Roads linking a settlement to your capital earn gold. Harbours earn gold and food and allow trade (and war) across the sea.
  • Trade routes pay both nations every turn and warm relations. Shared borders without trade, land hunger and warlike leaders cool them.
  • War must be declared (Other nations panel) before anyone can conquer. Conquering a settlement plunders it. Offer peace when you have had enough — the weaker or wearier side usually accepts, and a 10-turn truce follows. Castles defend everything within 2 tiles and raise your attack.
  • Edicts are national policies lasting 12 turns: Harvest Festival, Great Levy, Market Fairs or Corvée Labour.
  • Events — harvests, plagues, migrants, bandits, storms — strike every nation now and then. Castles keep bandits away.
  • Win by holding half of all claimed land, being the last nation standing, or having the highest score at the turn limit.
  • Drag / WASD / arrows to pan · scroll to zoom · C capital · L legend · G grid · Esc cancel.
