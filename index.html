🗺️ New World Map

A small turn-based nation builder on a procedurally generated world. Every nation — including yours — takes exactly one action per turn.

Choose your colour (each colour has its own national trait)

How to play

  • One action per turn. Click a tile, pick an action from the panel (or press Space to wait). Then every rival nation acts and the turn ends.
  • Tiles only produce when worked — within 2 tiles of one of your settlements. Found villages to bring new land into production.
  • Food feeds population; surplus grows your settlements (villages → towns → cities need population to upgrade). Shortfall causes famine.
  • Materials build improvements, roads, settlements and castles. Gold pays for expansion, trade and upkeep.
  • Resources (animals, ores, timber, crops, sea life) give a taste of their yield, and their full yield once you build the matching improvement: pasture, mine, lumber camp, farm or fishery.
  • Borders grow naturally around settlements each turn. Expand border grabs a specific tile now. Villages can also be founded on free land bordering your territory.
  • Roads connecting a settlement to your capital add gold. Harbours on coastal settlements add gold and food, and let you trade across the sea.
  • Trade routes earn both nations gold every turn and improve relations. Shared borders without trade breed friction, as do land-hungry or warlike neighbours.
  • Castles claim nearby land, defend everything within 2 tiles and add attack strength. Conquer an enemy border tile when your attack beats its defence.
  • Win by holding half of all claimed land, being the last nation standing, or having the highest score when the turn limit is reached.
  • Controls: drag or WASD/arrows to pan, scroll to zoom, C centre on capital, G grid, Esc deselect.
