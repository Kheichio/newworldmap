How to play

  • Everything is a card. Your hand (bottom of the map) holds up to 6 cards and refills at the start of each turn. Click a card, then Play, Keep it for later, or Discard it (free) to draw something else next turn.
  • Action cards — Expansion, Settlers, Charter, Farmers, Miners, Woodcutters, Herders, Fishers, Road Builders, Master Builder, Shipwrights, Merchants, Casus Belli, March, Treaty, Proclamation — spend an action point and follow the usual costs. Playing one highlights every valid tile (or opens a chooser for nations and edicts).
  • Bonus cards (Caravan, Tax Collectors, Prospectors, Bumper Crop, Migrants, Envoys, Festival, Rally) take effect at once and are free.
  • Actions per turn: 1, plus 1 for every two cities (up to 3). Press Space or End turn when you are done; then every rival moves.
  • The deck knows your nation. Cards tied to your colour's trait and your leader's trait are drawn more often (they glow gold). Cards with nothing to do right now are rare — but keeping a card you cannot use blocks a fresh draw, so discard freely.
  • Land only produces when worked — within 2 tiles of a settlement. Hatched tiles are yours but idle: play Settlers nearby.
  • Settlement limit: your capital supports 3 settlements; each town adds 1 and each city adds 2. Grow and upgrade (Charter) before you spread.
  • Food feeds your people; the surplus fills the growth bar and adds citizens. Villages become towns (4 people) and cities (9). Shortfalls cause famine. Materials build; gold pays for expansion, trade, war, edicts and upkeep.
  • Resources give a taste of their yield; the matching improvement unlocks it fully. Ruins reward whoever claims them.
  • War must be declared (Casus Belli) before anyone can March on enemy tiles. Conquered settlements are plundered. Offer peace with a Treaty; accepting an offer made to you is free. Castles defend everything within 2 tiles.
  • Sieges: a settlement usually needs two Marches — the first besieges it (halving its yields), the second within 4 turns takes it. Overwhelming force storms it outright.
  • Pacts (Pact card) make allies who cannot attack each other and receive a free Casus Belli when the other is attacked. Tribute: when clearly winning a war, a Treaty can demand tribute — peace plus part of their income for 20 turns.
  • Reaction cards (Militia, Sanctuary, Bribe, Ambush) are armed on your turn for free and trigger during the rivals' phase; unused ones return to your hand.
  • Market: buy a random card from a category for gold (two per turn). Mastery: playing the same card often improves it (★ on the card).
  • Combos: some cards get a bonus when played after another this turn — Road Builders after Settlers is free, an improvement after Expansion is half price, March after Casus Belli is +2 attack, Merchants after Envoys is free, Great Work after Prospectors is half price. The card shows "Combo ready" when it applies.
  • Hold & Mulligan: one card can be kept in the Hold slot outside your hand (safe from refills) and played later. Once per game, Mulligan throws your whole hand away for a fresh one.
  • Dilemmas (Refugees, Prophet, Border Dispute, Wandering Scholars, Bandit King) are free cards with two outcomes — you choose. Signature cards: each colour has a unique card only it can draw (Legion, Armada, Granary, Monopoly, Academy, Masons' Guild, Deep Mine, Wanderlust).
  • Faith: the Great Temple, a Prophet or a Revelation founds a faith named for your nation. It spreads along borders and trade routes; nations of the same faith warm to each other, different faiths cool, and attacking the faithful offends their kin. Founders gain contentment and score per follower.
  • Contentment (😊) comes from worked luxury resources (gems, spices, pearls, wine…), trade partners' luxuries, the Great Temple and festivals; sprawl and war lower it. Content people grow faster; below 30, settlements may rise up and go independent — claim them back with Expansion.
  • Wonders (Great Work card): five contributions at your capital complete one; each may exist only once in the world. Colonists and Sea Raids reach across the water from your harbours.
  • Edicts (Proclamation) are 12-turn policies. Events strike every nation now and then. The game autosaves every turn; unlocked cards carry over between games.
  • Win by holding half of all claimed land, being the last nation standing, or having the highest score at the turn limit.
  • Drag / WASD / arrows to pan · scroll to zoom · C capital · L legend · G grid · M mute · Esc cancel.
