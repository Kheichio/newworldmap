How to play

  • One action per turn. Use the quick actions (they highlight every valid tile), or click any tile and choose from its panel. Press Space to wait. Then every rival acts and the turn ends.
  • Land only produces when worked — within 2 tiles of one of your settlements. Hatched tiles on the map are yours but idle: found a village nearby to put them to work.
  • Food feeds population; the surplus fills the growth bar and adds people to your settlements. Villages become towns (pop 4) and cities (pop 9). A shortfall causes famine.
  • Materials build improvements, roads, settlements and castles. Gold pays for expansion, trade, conquest and upkeep. Costs rise as your nation grows.
  • Resources on a tile give a taste of their yield; the matching improvement (pasture, mine, lumber camp, farm, fishery) unlocks the full yield.
  • Borders grow by themselves around settlements every turn. Expand grabs a specific tile now. Villages can be founded on free land touching your border.
  • Roads that link a settlement to your capital earn gold. Harbours on coastal settlements earn gold and food and allow sea trade.
  • Trade routes pay both nations every turn and warm relations. Shared borders without trade, land hunger and warlike leaders cool them — hostile neighbours will attack.
  • Castles claim nearby land, defend everything within 2 tiles and raise your attack. Conquer an enemy border tile when your attack beats its defence (shown on the button).
  • Win by holding half of all claimed land, being the last nation standing, or having the highest score at the turn limit.
  • Drag / WASD / arrows to pan · scroll to zoom · C capital · L legend · G grid · Esc cancel.
